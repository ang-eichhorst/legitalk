Public Health Committee - 2026-02-05

Meetings

  • Call to Order and Opening Remarks (00:02:19)
    Chair McCarthy Vahey convened the Public Health Committee, welcomed members and new appointee Rep. Amy Romano, thanked staff and support teams, and previewed a challenging session ahead.
  • Introductions of Committee Staff and Support (00:03:21)
    Introductions of Beverly Henry (administrator), Diane Young-Rodney (clerk), assistant clerks Kathleen Panaza and Dave Radcliffe, LCO attorneys Eileen Lawler Parker and Nick Panzarella, OLR attorneys James Orlando and Nicole Dubey, David Kaplan, and intern Abigail Shaw.
  • Co-Chair Remarks and Tribute to Rep. Kevin Ryan (00:06:44)
    Sen. Saud Anwar highlighted the committee’s workload and unique emotional impact of public health testimony; offered a tribute to late former member Rep. Kevin Ryan and his decades of service.
  • Ranking Member Remarks (00:10:52)
    Rep. Nicole Klarides-Ditria thanked members and co-chairs, noted Rep. Kevin Ryan’s contributions, and emphasized continuing the committee’s work.
  • Moment of Silence for Rep. Kevin Ryan (00:11:33)
    The committee observed a moment of silence in memory of Rep. Ryan.
  • Concepts to be Raised: Reading of 18 Proposed Bill Titles (00:12:02)
    Chair read 18 concepts for potential bills, covering topics including patient safety plan transmission to schools, hospital sale-leasebacks and private equity attestations, hospital receivership, DDS revisions, social work licensure, EMS response times, overdose prevention, tobacco settlement funds, internet gaming disorder, veterinary medicine, omnibus public health revisions, dental medicine, medical marijuana access in facilities, Connecticut Valley Hospital patient protections, food-as-medicine for diabetes/CHF, student-athlete cardiac screening, and an endometriosis working group.
  • Motion to Raise Concepts and Discussion of Voting Procedure (00:14:10)
    A motion to raise all listed concepts was made and seconded; Q&A clarified that a voice vote would proceed and that detailed bill language would come later through drafting and public hearings.
  • Act Concerning the Secured Transmission of Minor Patient Safety Plans to Schools (concept) (00:16:13)
    Chair explained this builds on last year’s HB 7158 to allow, with consent, secure notification to schools of safety plans following youth suicidal ideation.
  • Act Concerning Hospital Sale-Leasebacks and Private Equity/Clinical Judgment Attestations (concept) (00:16:28)
    Modeled on 2023 SB 1507; addresses private equity control of hospitals and protects against interference with providers’ clinical judgment.
  • Act Requiring Evaluation of Appointing a Receiver for Hospitals in Financial Distress (concept) (00:16:49)
    Continues prior work to assess frameworks for hospital receivership when facilities face financial distress.
  • DDS Recommendations: Various Revisions to Developmental Services Statutes (concept) (00:16:54)
    Department proposals with technical and cleanup changes to developmental services statutes.
  • DDS Recommendations: Independent Mortality and Fatality Review Boards (concept) (00:16:59)
    Codifies and streamlines the Independent Mortality Review Board and Fatality Review Board, including alignment with a prior executive order and removal of duplications.
  • Act Concerning Social Work Licensure (LMSW Exam) (concept) (00:17:04)
    Addresses the LMSW exam sunset (01/01/2026) and national movement to eliminate the LMSW exam while retaining LCSW requirements.
  • Act Concerning EMS Response-Time Transparency (concept) (00:17:27)
    Aims to improve EMS data collection and transparency on response times, particularly in rural areas.
  • Act to Prevent Accidental Overdose Deaths and Improve Access to Treatment and Recovery (concept) (00:17:41)
    Revisits language worked on by Sen. Anwar regarding overdose prevention centers and broader overdose response strategies.
  • Act Concerning the Use of Tobacco Settlement Funds (concept) (00:17:49)
    Seeks to dedicate the fully recommended amount (approx. $32M+) to tobacco control/prevention as in prior years.
  • Act Concerning Internet Gaming Disorder, Gambling Risk, and Behavioral Health Prevention (concept) (00:18:07)
    Focuses on youth exposure to gambling-like behaviors via social media and online gaming, and related prevention efforts.
  • Act Concerning Veterinary Medicine (concept) (00:18:26)
    Explores licensure pathways for candidates trained out of state/abroad given the absence of an in-state veterinary school.
  • Act Concerning Various Revisions to the Public Health Statutes (Omnibus) (concept) (00:18:42)
    Consensus omnibus bill historically developed with ranking members; specific items still being screened and finalized.
  • Act Concerning Dental Medicine (concept) (00:19:08)
    Potential updates from the Dental Association and Oral Health Initiative; content under development pending further screening.
  • Act Requiring Certain Facilities to Allow Terminally Ill Patients to Use Medical Marijuana (concept) (00:19:26)
    Ensures facility policies allow access consistent with state law and navigates state–federal conflicts.
  • Act Concerning Patient Protections and Standards of Care at Connecticut Valley Hospital (concept) (00:19:48)
    Revives prior efforts (from Sen. Somers) to enhance patient safety within the hospital.
  • Act Concerning Nutrition and Food-Based Interventions for Diabetes and CHF (concept) (00:20:01)
    Builds on work by Sen. Anwar and Rep. Foster; examines hospital-based food-as-medicine interventions and coverage/payment mechanisms.
  • Act Concerning Cardiac Screening and Sudden Cardiac Arrest Prevention for Certain Student Athletes (concept) (00:20:34)
    Targets appropriate screening and risk assessment protocols for student athletes.
  • Act Establishing a Working Group Regarding Endometriosis (concept) (00:20:48)
    Codifies an existing ongoing effort; thanks offered to Reps. Gilchrest and Kennedy for leadership.
  • Additional Bill-Raising Sessions Anticipated (00:21:16)
    In response to a member question, the chair confirmed more concepts would be raised beyond today given significant forthcoming items (OHS, private equity, vaccines).
  • Voice Vote and Attendance Window (00:22:35)
    Committee took a voice vote to raise the concepts; meeting left open for attendance until House session at noon.
  • Scheduling Announcements and Recess (00:23:18)
    Announced upcoming committee meeting (9th at noon) to raise more titles and first public hearing (18th); meeting recessed until noon.
