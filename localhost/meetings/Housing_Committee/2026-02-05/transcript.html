Legitalk:

Housing Committee - 2026-02-05

Meetings

  • Item 1 — An Act Appropriating Funds to Assist Homeless Persons (00:11:41)
    House Chair Felipe introduced a recurring concept to signal bipartisan support for increased homelessness funding to Appropriations (historically $30–50M). Rep. Scott asked about current funding levels (~$15–20M) and accountability; Felipe agreed to include safeguards and noted cold/warm weather shelter needs. Sen. Sampson supported moving the concept while emphasizing funds should reach individuals rather than administrative overhead. Adopted on voice vote.
  • Item 2 — An Act Concerning the Reporting of Identifying Information of Nonresident Landlords (00:17:50)
    Chair Felipe explained the goal is to ensure a direct, reliable point of contact for landlords who do not reside at the property, especially for emergencies (not to pierce corporate liability). Rep. Scott sought definitions of 'nonresident' (not residing in the building/parcel) and asked how this builds on last year’s public act requiring contact info; Felipe said they want more than a generic email to ensure reachability. Sen. Sampson supported the concept as long as it doesn’t pierce the corporate veil. Rep. Kavros DeGraw asked about municipal collection; Felipe noted many clerks already do and this would set a statewide requirement. Adopted on voice vote.
  • Item 3 — An Act Relative to the Education of Homeless Children and Youth (McKinney-Vento Codification) (00:25:20)
    Chair Felipe proposed codifying the federal McKinney-Vento protections to ensure students experiencing homelessness can remain in their school of origin if federal policy changes. Rep. Scott supported but cautioned against spending limited session time codifying federal law absent imminent change. Adopted on voice vote.
  • Item 4 — An Act Concerning Rental Assistance Program (RAP) Home Inspections (00:27:18)
    Chair Felipe summarized a concept to align RAP inspections more flexibly with Section 8 practices, allowing landlords to cure minor issues (e.g., a missing drawer knob or one nonworking burner) without failing and delaying tenancy, to keep landlords participating. Rep. Gonzalez asked if tenant-caused issues matter; Felipe clarified inspections occur pre-move-in. Sen. Sampson supported reducing delays that cause landlords to move on to other tenants. Adopted on voice vote.
  • Item 5 — An Act Concerning Bond Authorization for the Connecticut Housing Finance Authority (CHFA) (00:30:25)
    Chair Felipe proposed allowing CHFA’s existing Emergency Mortgage Assistance Program bond authorization to be used for down payment assistance. Sen. Sampson, noting his real-estate background, requested clarity on whether funds would be grants or other assistance and raised concerns that large DPA grants can inflate entry-level home prices. Chair Felipe agreed to consult CHFA and report details back. Adopted on voice vote.
