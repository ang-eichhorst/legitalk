Higher Education and Employment Committee - 2026-02-05

Meetings

  • Call to Order and Temporary Recess (00:01:48)
    Sen. Slap opens the first meeting of the session for the Higher Education and Employment Advancement Committee and briefly recesses to wait for House colleagues to conclude floor session.
  • Reconvene and Staff Introductions (00:12:08)
    Meeting reconvenes; Sen. Slap introduces policy staff and nonpartisan staff supporting the committee this session.
  • Concept to Raise: Act Prohibiting Scholarship Displacement (Item 1) (00:14:01)
    Chair explains the proposal to prohibit institutions from reducing institutional aid when students receive outside scholarships, noting prior committee action and stakeholder work.
  • Concept to Raise: Act Implementing Recommendations of the Task Force to Support Promise Programs (Item 2) (00:14:56)
    Chair outlines recommendations from last year's Promise Programs task force, including support for existing programs, seeding new programs, and building a statewide Promise ecosystem.
  • Concept to Raise: Act Implementing UConn’s Plan to Join a Leading Association of Research Universities (Item 3) (00:16:01)
    Rep. Haddad describes aligning with the standards of top research university associations (e.g., AAU), incorporating recommendations to enhance UConn’s research profile and competitiveness for federal research funding.
  • Concept to Raise: LCO Technical Revisions to Higher Education Statutes (Item 6) (00:17:07)
    Annual technical revisions package from the Legislative Commissioners’ Office to update and clarify higher education statutes.
  • Concept to Raise: Promotion of Learn-and-Earn Paid Work-Based Learning Opportunities (Item 7) (00:17:22)
    Rep. Haddad presents a state strategy to expand paid internships and work-based learning, aligning with NEBHE’s regional 10,000-internship challenge to improve student outcomes and in-state retention.
  • Concept to Raise: Journalism Fellowship Program for Graduates (Item 8) (00:18:30)
    Rep. Farrar outlines a fellowship program to strengthen local news organizations while creating career pathways for recent graduates, modeled on successful programs in other states.
  • Voice Vote: Raise Concepts (Items 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8) (00:21:47)
    Committee approves raising six concepts via voice vote; Chair clarifies that raising a concept is not a vote on final passage.
  • Placeholder Bills: Act Concerning Postsecondary Education (Item 4) and Act Concerning Workforce Development (Item 5) (00:22:16)
    Chair introduces placeholder titles for separate consideration; a roll call is conducted and votes are left open until 4:00 PM to accommodate members.
  • Vote to Draft SB 8 — An Act Supporting Graduate Students in the State (00:24:49)
    Committee votes to draft SB 8 to address the federal Grad PLUS program cancellation by developing a state-backed alternative with CHESLA, aiming to preserve approximately $90 million in affordable graduate student financing for fields including nursing and teaching.
  • Announcements: Next Committee Meeting and Upcoming Deadlines (00:26:13)
    Next meeting set for February 17 at 11:30 AM; expected to be the last opportunity to raise additional bill titles and to set the date for the first public hearing.
  • Recess to Keep Roll-Call Votes Open (00:27:21)
    Committee recesses for one hour, leaving the roll call open until 4:00 PM for items 4 and 5.
