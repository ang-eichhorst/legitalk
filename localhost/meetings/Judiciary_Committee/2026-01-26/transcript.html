Judiciary Committee - 2026-01-26

Meetings

  • Approval of June meeting minutes (00:00:16)
    The committee moved, seconded, and unanimously approved adopting the June meeting minutes.
  • Subcommittee updates (Visioning and Governance) (00:01:11)
    Ben reported limited updates but emphasized setting expectations for working with the interim ombuds, while Marisol outlined Governance’s review of the prior job description and support for prioritizing lived experience over formal degrees.
  • Interim Ombuds update (00:04:07)
    The chair had no official update; Marisol shared secondhand information that two candidates had been interviewed, and members agreed to keep pressing appointing authorities.
  • Revise and approve Ombuds job description and posting (00:06:41)
    The committee reviewed tracked changes (clarifying litigation authority from statute, changing 'private grants' to 'external grants', and removing bachelor’s degree preference), agreed to treat qualifications as preferred to broaden applicants, set a September 30 deadline, and unanimously approved posting.
  • Public Comment (00:50:23)
    Barbara Fair urged strong commitment and questioned preferring state-government experience, and a formerly incarcerated commenter highlighted legal access issues and expressed interest in applying; the committee confirmed preference for impacted applicants and explained the upcoming posting process.
  • Adjournment and next steps (01:15:28)
    The meeting adjourned with the plan to forward the revised job description for posting and begin receiving applications by late July/early August, with a September 30 application deadline.
