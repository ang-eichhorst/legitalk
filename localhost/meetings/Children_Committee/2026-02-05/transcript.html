Children Committee - 2026-02-05

Meetings

  • Call to Order and Opening Remarks (00:16:44)
    Committee on Children convened at 11:03 AM on Thursday, 02/05/2026. House Chair Representative Perez called the meeting to order, introduced Senate Co-Chair Senator Ceci Maher, recognized House Ranking Member Representative Anne Dauphinais, and acknowledged Vice Chairs Representative Mary Welander and Senator Christine Cohen. Senator Perillo was welcomed as the new Senate Ranking Member. Voting window logistics were set to remain open until 12:00 PM due to session timing.
  • Act requiring certain agencies to report costs associated with programs serving children and families (ref. SB 1214 (2025)) (00:19:56)
    Re-raises a prior concept (most recently SB 1214 in 2025) to require the Departments of Social Services and Developmental Services to report current and projected costs of programs serving children and families, including impacts of expanding eligibility thresholds. Passed via voice vote.
  • Task force on methods and programs for improving affordability for children and families (ref. HB 6841 (2025)) (00:21:35)
    Re-raises a 2025 House-passed, bipartisan bill to create a task force to study poverty reduction, program availability, barriers to alleviating poverty, and regional cost impacts. Motion made and seconded; passed via voice vote.
  • DCF recommendations bill (00:22:36)
    Department of Children and Families proposal to consolidate several reports into one annual report and clarify notification requirements to law enforcement for missing children. Motion made and seconded; passed via voice vote.
  • Child welfare accountability and transparency (HB 5004) (00:23:21)
    House priority bill to strengthen accountability and ensure adequate resources within the child welfare system. Designated to become HB 5004. Passed via voice vote.
  • Voluntary infant surrender task force (00:24:06)
    Establishes a task force to review the state's voluntary infant surrender law (adopted ~2000) with updated perspectives, including potential improvements to access and utilization. Members noted interest in evaluating 'baby box' secure surrender units; several legislators voiced support to include these devices within the task force scope. Motion made and seconded; passed via voice vote.
  • Child care subsidies (Care 4 Kids benefits cliff) (00:29:11)
    Addresses benefit cliffs in the Care 4 Kids child care subsidy program to better support working families. Advocates report bipartisan interest across chambers; details to be developed. Passed via voice vote.
  • Municipal camp safety (00:30:02)
    Proposal to implement mandated reporter training (free, online) at all municipal camps without municipal cost, and to convene a working group to assess the scope, types, and needs of municipal camps statewide. Questions raised about potential burdens on short-duration camps; concept advanced via voice vote.
  • Placeholder: An Act Concerning Children's Safety (Agenda Item 8) (00:34:14)
    One of four placeholder concepts (Items 8–11). Due to vagueness, members requested roll call rather than voice vote. Proceeded to roll call with votes held open until 12:00 PM; some members indicated opposition or passes pending specifics, others voted yes.
  • Placeholder: An Act Concerning Children's Well-Being (Agenda Item 9) (00:34:20)
    Second of four placeholder measures. Advanced to roll call with recorded votes; some minority members opposed the placeholders due to lack of detail, while others supported raising them.
  • Placeholder: An Act Concerning Children's Health (Agenda Item 10) (00:34:24)
    Third placeholder measure. Included with Items 8–11 in a combined roll call; votes left open until 12:00 PM. Additional yes votes were later recorded by phone.
  • Placeholder: An Act Concerning Children's Programs (Agenda Item 11) (00:34:27)
    Fourth placeholder measure in the set. Advanced via roll call with votes held open; subsequent phone-in confirmations included yes votes from Representatives Kitt and Boyd and a senator.
  • Recess, voting window, and next meeting notice (00:35:57)
    Committee recessed with votes held open until 12:00 PM. Next meeting set for February 17 at 10:00 AM in Room 1C with hearing at 12:00 PM. During the open voting window, additional roll call confirmations were recorded by phone from Representative Kitt (yes on Items 8–11), Representative Boyd (yes on Items 8–11), and a senator later identified as Senator Marks (yes).
